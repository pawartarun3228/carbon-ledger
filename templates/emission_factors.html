Academic & Benchmark Traceability Notice
All conversion factors in this prototype are grounded in documented public standards. Grid electricity is referenced to the Central Electricity Authority (CEA) CO₂ Baseline Database for the Indian Power Sector (v19) combined margin baseline. Stationary and mobile liquid fuels follow IPCC 2006 Guidelines (Vol 2, Energy). Commuter and rail passenger transport benchmarks follow UK DEFRA / DESNZ 2023 conversion coefficients. These factors are provided for software demonstration and educational testing.

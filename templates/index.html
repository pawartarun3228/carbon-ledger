System Architecture

Four Methodological Pillars

Designed to bridge deterministic environmental accounting standards with rigorous machine learning and policy scenario simulation.

1. Verifiable Accounting

Rigorous E = Activity × Emission Factor computation with explicit unit compatibility. Preserves factor provenance, CEA national grid baselines, and IPCC fuel coefficients for reproducibility.

2. ML Forecasting Lab

Chronological holdout benchmarking comparing Naive persistence, Linear Regression, and Random Forest Regressors without future-data leakage.

3. Explainable Advisory

Evidence-based decarbonization recommendations driven by verified emissions data. Includes causal rationales, abatement potential, and stated assumptions.

4. Net-Zero Pathways

Interactive decarbonization scenario planner that recalculates emissions from activity interventions and projects annual glide-path trajectories to 2030–2050 targets.
